Introduction

Users who click on the hotspot are redirected to the linked page or content.

The Image Hotspots app empowers e-commerce merchants to enhance their product images with interactive hotspots, enabling customers to access additional information and highlighting key product features.

Benefits

The Dynamic Image Hotspot feature provides an engaging way to present product details, enhancing user interaction and improving customer experience. By offering clickable areas with additional information, it helps merchants effectively showcase their products and drive customer engagement.

Steps to Use the Dynamic Image Hotspot

Adding Hotspots to Images

  • Step 1: Open the website where you want to add image hotspots.
  • Step 2: Click on the edit button to enter editing mode.
  • Step 3: Access the Hotspot tool on the left-hand side of the editor, as shown in the illustration below.
  • Step 4: Drag and drop the hotspot element from the sidebar onto the page. The hotspot will appear on the image.

Adding Hotspots to Any Image

  • Step 1: To add a hotspot to a background image, double-click the image.
  • Step 2: Click on the image where you want to add the hotspot. Options will appear on the left-hand side of the editor.
  • Step 3: Add a hotspot to the selected image and configure the position as desired.
